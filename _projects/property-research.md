## The business context

The business began as a home-cooked tiffin service offering meals based on individual diet plans, then expanded into homestay and food services. I supported its research, promotional content, customer information and trip-planning needs for approximately one year.

## My contribution

I independently wrote scraping code and supplemented automated collection with manual research. I also edited owner-supplied photographs, added graphics, created promotional and customer information materials, made basic testimonial-video edits and planned itineraries for actual customers.

## Property research

I used Python, Selenium and Scrapy to collect property details, calendar information and pricing where available. The scraper ran successfully and produced a usable dataset in Excel sheets.

## Creative and customer support

Using Canva and Adobe tools, I created a logo, social graphics, brochures, food menu cards and homestay-rules graphics. I worked from photographs supplied by the owner, editing them and adding graphics for the business materials.

I also prepared itineraries for actual customers and made basic edits to customer-feedback and testimonial videos.

## What I delivered

- Property-research scraping code and Excel datasets.
- Logo, menu cards, brochures and social graphics.
- Homestay-rules and customer-information materials.
- Customer itineraries and basic testimonial-video edits.

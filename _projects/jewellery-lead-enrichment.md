## The business need

A jewellery-sector client needed improvements to an existing list of approximately 2,000 business records and additional prospects covering retailers and manufacturers. The starting information included business names, addresses and postal codes; the requested research covered contact details, websites and address information.

## My contribution

I personally developed the Python scripts, carried out the research, automated prospect collection, cleaned the data and prepared the Excel delivery.

## Research and enrichment

I built a row-by-row workflow using Python, Selenium and pandas. The workflow used the available business name or address to search through Chrome, research business websites and business listings, and collect website and email information.

For additional prospect discovery, I used Python automation to research public directories with combinations of industry keywords and city names. I used Google Sheets functions to clean up the collected data and handle duplicates.

## What I delivered

- Research and enrichment of an existing list of approximately 2,000 records.
- Collection of approximately 2,000 or more additional business prospects.
- Cleanup and duplicate handling across the collected information.
- Excel files for the client's use.

## Client outcome

The project was completed and the client accepted the Excel delivery.
